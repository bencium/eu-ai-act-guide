# User journey: synthetic brand ambassador video campaign

1. A Spanish cosmetics brand's agency generates a 30-second video of a
   photorealistic woman recommending a face cream, using a text-to-video
   model. The person does not exist but looks like a real influencer filmed
   in a bathroom.
2. The video is published on the brand's public Instagram, TikTok and
   YouTube channels and runs as paid advertising across the EU.
3. Neither the caption nor the video itself states that the person or
   footage is AI-generated. A small "#digitalcreation" hashtag appears as
   the 14th of 17 hashtags in the caption.
4. The agency exported the file through the model provider's API, which the
   provider says embeds invisible provenance metadata; nobody at the agency
   has verified this survives Instagram's re-encoding.
5. The brand's legal team approved the script but never saw the final video.
