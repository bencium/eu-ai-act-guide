# User journey: instant loan decision at an Irish consumer lender

1. A consumer in Dublin applies for a €4,000 personal loan through a lender's
   mobile app.
2. The app asks for consent to read 12 months of open-banking transaction
   data, then displays "Analysing your finances…".
3. A machine-learning model built in-house scores creditworthiness from the
   transaction history, declared income, and device metadata. The model was
   put into service in March 2027.
4. Applications scoring above a threshold are approved instantly and funds
   are sent the same day. Applications below a second threshold are declined
   instantly with the message "We can't offer you a loan at this time."
5. Mid-band applications go to a human underwriter queue.
6. Declined applicants can reapply after 90 days; the app offers no
   explanation of the decision and no route to request human review.
