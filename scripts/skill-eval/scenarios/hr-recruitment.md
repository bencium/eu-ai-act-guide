# User journey: CV screening at a Dutch logistics company

1. A hiring manager at a 900-employee logistics company in Rotterdam opens the
   company's applicant tracking system to fill three warehouse-supervisor roles.
2. The ATS includes a "Smart Ranking" feature the company bought from a US
   vendor. It reads each submitted CV, scores candidates from 0 to 100 against
   the job description, and orders the applicant list by score.
3. The manager reviews only the top 15 of 212 applicants, as the interface
   shows the rest under a collapsed "lower match" section.
4. Two candidates are auto-flagged "employment gap risk" by the same feature.
   The manager rejects both without opening their CVs.
5. Rejected applicants receive a templated email that does not mention any
   automated scoring.
6. The company has no documented process for a human to re-check the ranking,
   and the vendor documentation was never reviewed by HR.
