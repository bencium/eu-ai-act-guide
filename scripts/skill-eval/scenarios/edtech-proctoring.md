# User journey: remote exam proctoring with engagement detection

1. A university in Munich requires students to install a proctoring app for
   remote exams.
2. During the exam the app records webcam video and analyses the student's
   face continuously.
3. The vendor brochure says the system detects "stress, confusion and
   distraction levels" from facial expression and gaze, and produces an
   "integrity confidence score" per student.
4. Scores below a threshold flag the recording for staff review, and flagged
   students are invited to a misconduct interview.
5. Students are told recording occurs, but not that emotional or attention
   states are inferred.
6. The university signed the vendor contract in 2025; the current model
   version was deployed by the vendor in September 2026.
